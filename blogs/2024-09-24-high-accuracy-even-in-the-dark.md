Plumerai Familiar Face Identification enables smart home cameras to notify you when loved ones arrive safely, activate floodlights if a stranger loiters in your yard, and quickly search through recordings. It’s also a critical building block for the vision LLM features that we’re developing. As shown in the video, it performs exceptionally well even in complete darkness, accurately and effortlessly identifying people.
