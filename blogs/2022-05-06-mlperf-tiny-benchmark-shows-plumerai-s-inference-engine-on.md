We recently announced Plumerai’s participation in MLPerf Tiny , the best-known public benchmark suite for evaluation of machine learning inference tools and methods. In the latest v0.7 of the MLPerf Tiny results, we participated along with 7 other companies. The published results confirm the claims that we made earlier on our blog : our inference engine is indeed the world’s fastest on Arm Cortex-M microcontrollers.
