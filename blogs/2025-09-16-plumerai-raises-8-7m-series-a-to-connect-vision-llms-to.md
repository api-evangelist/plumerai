Press release London – September 16, 2025 - Plumerai™, a pioneer in on-device AI for cameras, today announced an $8.7M Series A funding round, led by new investors Partech and OTB Ventures, with support from Acclimate Ventures and existing investors. This brings the total funding received to over $17M. The new funding will drive Plumerai’s ambition to enable trillions of intelligent devices, a future that is now accelerated by its new Vision LLM features.
