We have launched Automatic Enrollment for our Plumerai Familiar Face Identification! Automatic Enrollment makes it effortless to register new individuals for identification; as soon as a person walks up to the camera, they will be automatically registered, and users can tag them with a name in the app. This feature: identifies people from an 8m/26ft distance (often beyond), excels with a variety of camera positions, camera angles, and in low light conditions, and most importantly, it has been built to run on the device (not in the cloud!), so it doesn’t compromise on privacy.
