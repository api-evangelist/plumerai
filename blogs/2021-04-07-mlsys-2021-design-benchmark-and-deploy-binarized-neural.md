We are very excited to present our paper Larq Compute Engine: Design, Benchmark, and Deploy State-of-the-Art Binarized Neural Networks at the MLSys 2021 conference this week! Larq Compute Engine (LCE) is a state-of-the-art inference engine for Binarized Neural Networks (BNNs). LCE makes it possible for researchers to easily benchmark BNNs on mobile devices.
