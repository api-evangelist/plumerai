One of our engineers, Cedric Nugteren, recently set himself an unusual challenge: running modern AI on a computer from 1985. In the video below, Cedric introduces the Atari ST, a system he first programmed as a 6-year old, and revisits it as a hobby project decades later. He demonstrates his MidiSurf game , and shows how he successfully ported Plumerai People Detection to the Atari ST.
