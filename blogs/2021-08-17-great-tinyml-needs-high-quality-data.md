So far we have mostly written about how we enable AI applications on tiny hardware by using Binarized Neural Networks (BNNs). The use of BNNs helps us to reduce the required memory, the inference latency and energy consumption of our AI models, but there is something that we have been less vocal about that is at least as important for AI in the real world: high-quality training data. To train tiny models, choose your data wisely Deep learning models are famously hungry for training data, as more training data is usually the most effective way to improve accuracy.
